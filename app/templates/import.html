Export your Google Sheet as CSV (File → Download → Comma-separated values) and drop it here. The columns get matched up automatically and you confirm the mapping before anything is written.

How rows are read

  • Rows that name the same artist collapse into one artist with several wishlist items — that’s the thing a flat sheet can’t do.
  • An artist already in the library is matched by name and added to, not duplicated.
  • Tags split on commas, semicolons, slashes and pipes.
  • Prices are stripped of currency symbols. bought, sold out, passed and similar words in a status column are recognised.
  • A link with no item attached becomes one of the artist’s links.
